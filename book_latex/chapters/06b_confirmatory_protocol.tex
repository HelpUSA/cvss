\chapter{Confirmatory Validation Protocol (G10B)}
\label{chap:g10b_confirmatory}

\section{Purpose and Freeze Boundary}
The deterministic HelpUS CVSS v4.0 baseline is frozen at commit
\texttt{007fe79a6cd0e843fe6a9f837444720e23a4bcad}. The held-out
confirmatory datasets C1 and C2 remain strictly sealed and cannot be used for
debugging, calibration, rule selection, or threshold tuning.

The confirmatory evaluation is governed by
\texttt{HELPUS-G10B-CONFIRMATORY-RUN-v1.0}. Architectural freeze does not
authorize dataset unsealing. A separate explicit authorization by the Master
SuperAdmin (\texttt{wmsjp}) is required after all execution artifacts and
custody bindings are frozen.

\section{Fail-Closed Execution Contract}
The gatekeeper \texttt{scripts/g10b\_confirmatory\_gate.py} enforces the
authorization boundary before any real confirmatory execution. The gate binds
the authorization record to the protocol hash, executor hash, dependency-lock
hash, frozen baseline, and pre-registered custody hashes for C1 and C2.

The primary run is deterministic-core only. Network access is disabled during
inference and HCAL is disabled. C1 and C2 are executed as one governed
single-pass transaction; raw or aggregate results are not disclosed until both
datasets complete and the output evidence is cryptographically sealed.

\section{Pre-Registered Acceptance Criteria}
For C1, primary acceptance requires metric-level exact agreement of at least
95\%, full-vector exact agreement of at least 90\%, MAE no greater than 0.25
CVSS points, maximum absolute error no greater than 1.0, severity-band
agreement of at least 95\%, zero forced guesses where abstention is required,
and zero canonical scoring-engine arithmetic divergences.

For C2, the confirmatory requirement is zero violations across all
pre-registered metamorphic invariants, zero state/order leakage, zero
unexpected crashes, zero forced guesses when abstention is required, and zero
deterministic reproducibility failures.

\section{Irreversibility of Exposure}
Once at least one held-out benchmark record has been parsed or scored, the
confirmatory exposure is consumed. Any subsequent retuning of rules, scoring
semantics, parser behavior, or acceptance thresholds prevents reuse of C1/C2
for a new confirmatory claim. A new independent held-out dataset is then
required.

\section{Reporting Boundary}
C1/C2 results apply only to the exact frozen deterministic baseline tested in
the run manifest. They do not constitute FIRST certification or endorsement.
HCAL remains a secondary optional layer and, if evaluated later, its results
must be reported separately as post-hoc analysis.
