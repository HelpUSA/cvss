\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This work presents an autonomous-by-default, evidence-constrained framework for
CVSS v4.0 environmental contextualization in which final score calculation is
deterministic and auditable. The engineering baseline has been frozen after
calculator conformance, reproducibility, rule-catalog validation, synthetic
metamorphic testing, and clean-room checks.

The primary scientific claim is intentionally narrower than autonomous expert
replacement: given structured and verifiable environmental evidence, the
system can derive candidate environmental/modified metrics under explicit
rules, abstain when evidence is insufficient, and reproduce the same
FIRST-compatible deterministic result from the same inputs.

The remaining primary validation step is the pre-registered G10B held-out
confirmatory run over C1 and C2. Those datasets remain sealed until explicit
authorization and a one-time clean-room execution. Any later retuning after
confirmatory exposure requires a new independent held-out dataset for a new
confirmatory claim.

HCAL remains a secondary optional assurance architecture rather than a runtime
dependency of the deterministic core. Future work may compare alternative
policy engines, richer provider-neutral context schemas, and optional human
expert studies without retroactively changing the frozen confirmatory
baseline.
