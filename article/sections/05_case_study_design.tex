\section{Study Design}
\label{sec:case-study}

The primary study uses non-human technical validation. The unit of analysis is
a vulnerability-plus-environment evidence package evaluated by the frozen
deterministic HelpUS pipeline. Human expert adjudication is not a prerequisite
for the primary study and is retained only as an optional future comparative
extension.

Development evidence is separated from confirmatory evidence. Dataset B is
used for architecture development, regression testing, and provenance checks.
Synthetic/metamorphic campaigns exercise deterministic invariants and
fail-safe behavior. The held-out datasets C1 and C2 are reserved for a
single-pass blind confirmatory run under the G10B protocol and are not used for
debugging or calibration.

Each development or synthetic case can contain:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item a canonical CVSS v4.0 source vector;
  \item structured asset and reachability evidence;
  \item organizational security-requirement policy evidence;
  \item vulnerability-specific exploit preconditions;
  \item subsequent-system impact evidence where applicable; and
  \item provenance, freshness, and conflict metadata.
\end{itemize}

The deterministic pipeline may auto-accept a metric decision only when the
required evidence predicates are satisfied. Missing, stale, or materially
conflicting evidence routes to warning, review, or abstention rather than a
forced guess. Confidence may affect routing but never the official CVSS score.

The primary evaluation measures specification conformance, exact
metric/vector agreement where an oracle exists, deterministic reproducibility,
score error, abstention safety, evidence provenance, and metamorphic invariant
preservation. HCAL is outside the primary endpoint and, if evaluated, is
reported separately as a secondary post-hoc assurance layer.
