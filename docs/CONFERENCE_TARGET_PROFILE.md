# Conference target profile based on ICITEICS-2026

## Decision

ICITEICS-2026 is a useful model for requirements, but the July 2026 deadline is too close for a strong submission. The paper should be strengthened for a later IEEE-style conference.

## Captured requirements

- English manuscript and presentation.
- Microsoft CMT submission.
- IEEE format.
- 5 to 8 pages.
- Maximum 6 authors.
- Double-blind review with at least 3 reviewers.
- Plagiarism checking.
- Accepted and presented papers submitted for IEEE Xplore inclusion.
- Mandatory presentation, hybrid mode.
- Original, unpublished work; no simultaneous submission; avoid self-copying.

## Captured dates

- Submission: 10 July 2026.
- Acceptance notification: 22 July 2026.
- Camera-ready: 10 August 2026.
- Early bird registration: 15 August 2026.
- Conference: 09 to 10 October 2026.

## Double-blind implications

Remove or anonymize author names, affiliations, emails, public GitHub links, HelpUSA, Wagner, acknowledgments, and any public dashboard URL that identifies authorship. Use anonymized artifact wording during review and restore identities only after acceptance.

## Target paper posture

Next submission should be a 7 to 8 page IEEE-style paper on:

> An AI-assisted watcher workflow for evidence-backed CVSS v4.0 Environmental metric assessment.

The paper needs clear CVSS v4.0 alignment, curated scenarios, traceability artifacts, quantitative result summaries, explicit limitations, and no production-grade or predictive-superiority claims.
