## 1. Review of Gemini proposals

Full review:

`docs/architecture/POST_FREEZE_CONTEXT_POLICY_ROADMAP.md`

Key decisions:

1. Provider-neutral canonical context model: **ACCEPT**, with mandatory
   provenance, authority, freshness, scope, conflict state, and separate asset,
   vulnerability, evidence, control, and policy contexts.
2. Lattice/partial-order resolution: **ACCEPT WITH CORRECTION**. Specificity
   alone is insufficient; incomparable material evidence must route to
   ABSTAIN/review.
3. Hash-chained ledger: **ACCEPT AS HARDENING**, but add signed/external
   checkpoints; hash chaining alone is tamper-evident, not immutable.
4. OPA/Rego: **DEFER AS AUTHORITATIVE ENGINE**. Use shadow/conformance mode
   first; any rule-engine replacement creates a new governed baseline.
5. Datalog: optional formal research alternative.
6. ABSTAIN contract: **ACCEPT** as HelpUS workflow/evidence state, never as a
   CVSS metric value.
7. Bounded score interval: **ACCEPT ONLY AS HELPUS UNCERTAINTY ENVELOPE**,
   computed from legally admissible assignments through the unchanged scorer.
8. `AR:L` from stateless/HA: **REJECT**. AR is an organizational Security
   Requirement, not a redundancy metric.
9. `MAC:H` from a generic effective WAF: **REJECT** unless the exact CVSS
   Attack Complexity semantic predicate is proven for the vulnerability.
10. MR-1..MR-4: **ACCEPT WITH STRONGER PRECONDITIONS**. MR-5..MR-8 are proposed
    for future post-freeze R&D.
