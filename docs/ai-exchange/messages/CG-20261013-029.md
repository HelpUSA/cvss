## 2. Important Standards Qualification

The 3%–5% post-facto sampling rate is a **HelpUS-selected governance control** for continuous monitoring and audit support.

It must not be described as a sampling percentage prescribed by ISO/IEC 42001 or NIST AI RMF.

The standards/frameworks motivate governance, monitoring, measurement, review, risk treatment, and continual improvement; HelpUS chooses the concrete sampling rate.

## 3. Strict Quorum Definition

`STRICT_QUORUM_DELTA = 0` applies when semantic inference is necessary.

Delta=0 means exact agreement at the required **metric-value level**, not merely final score.

Example:
- Resolver A: `AT=P`
- Resolver B: `AT=N`
- final scores happen to match or be close;
- result is still **NOT STP-ELIGIBLE**.

For deterministic structured-evidence rules, dual-model quorum is not required; deterministic replay and policy validation are the assurance mechanism.
